RS-02 · Mechanism

Jansen Walker

A four-legged Theo Jansen linkage walker, printed on my own machines. Two small gearbox motors turn a crank, and the legs turn that rotation into a walk.

In progress

The build

  • Four Jansen legs driven by two TT gearbox motors
  • 9V battery and a speed controller
  • A 3D-printed chain whose joints are pins of 1.75 mm filament

The rivets

The chain links are tiny: 5.51 mm between the two holes on a plate. To pin them I had PCBWay machine a brass soldering-iron tip with a small dome-shaped cavity. Pressed onto a stub of filament, it forms a rivet head. Too much heat smears the plastic, so the iron runs low.
